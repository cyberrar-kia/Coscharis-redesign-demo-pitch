Group Profile

Nearly five decades of Nigerian industry.

Coscharis Group began in 1977 with the importation and distribution of genuine automotive spare parts. That foundation grew into the sole Nigerian franchise of BMW, and later Jaguar Land Rover, Ford, MINI and — as only the second franchisee in Africa — Rolls-Royce.
