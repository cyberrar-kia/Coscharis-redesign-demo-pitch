Group Profile

Nearly five decades of Nigerian industry.

Coscharis Group began in 1977 with the importation and distribution of genuine automotive spare parts. That foundation grew into the sole Nigerian franchise of BMW, and later Jaguar Land Rover, Ford, MINI and — as only the second franchisee in Africa — Rolls-Royce.

Today the Group's interests reach well beyond the showroom floor: information technology, healthcare distribution, rice farming in Anambra, bottled water and yoghurt production, Ford vehicle assembly, and operations across Ghana — all under one founder-led vision to "build an institution that will be timeless in its relevance."
