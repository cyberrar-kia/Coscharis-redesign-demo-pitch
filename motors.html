Coscharis Motors

Motors

The Group's flagship — sole Nigerian dealer for BMW, Rolls-Royce, Jaguar Land Rover, MINI and Ford.

Since incorporation in 1977, Coscharis Motors PLC — our flagship company — has been driven by the vision of remaining timeless in relevance and value. By constantly keeping to our brand promise, Coscharis Motors has become a household name providing the Nigerian people with vehicles from a robustly diversified auto dealership, comprising five luxury and two value brands, showcased in ultra-modern showrooms nationwide.

Coscharis Motors has made significant investments in state-of-the-art aftersales facilities across the country and trained specialized personnel to ensure customers stay satisfied while putting their vehicles on the road. Through strategic partnerships, Coscharis Motors ensures that wherever you are, our services are readily accessible.

Beyond the brands Coscharis Motors represents, the company also supplies genuine automotive spare parts and related products to the general public via distributors. Sales and service locations span the five geopolitical zones, including Abuja, Kano, Gombe, Lagos, Ekiti, Ibadan, Port Harcourt, Uyo, Calabar and Enugu, among others.
