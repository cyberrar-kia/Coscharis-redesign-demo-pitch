Coscharis Motors Assembly

Motors Assembly

Assembling the Ford Ranger in Ikeja — Nigeria's only Ford assembly location outside South Africa.

With the intent to support the growth of the Nigerian automotive industry, Coscharis Motors Assembly Ltd (CMAL), established in 2014, commenced assembling Ford Rangers (pickup trucks) with its first units rolled out in November 2015. This development makes Nigeria the only location outside of South Africa where Ford assembles cars in Africa.

CMAL is a subsidiary of Coscharis Motors with the status of assembler of Ford vehicles for the Nigerian market. Ford Motor Company reinforced its partnership by availing Coscharis fully-fitted equipment and technical support for the Semi Knocked Down (SKD) plant, as well as training local personnel to handle the assembly plant.

The CMAL plant is located in an SKD facility in PWD, Ikeja; its registered office and main plant for the SKD and CKD is sited at Km 32 Lekki Epe Expressway, Awoyaya, Lagos, on a piece of land in excess of 14 hectares. At Coscharis Motors Assembly Ltd, it is our commitment to stakeholders to set standards that other players in the industry will be judged by.
