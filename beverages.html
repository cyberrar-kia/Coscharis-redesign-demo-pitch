Coscharis Beverages

Beverages

Table water, sachet water and yoghurt production from Lagos and Anambra, distributed nationwide since 2002.

Coscharis Beverages was founded with a vision to deliver clean, safe-to-consume water and other beverages at the most competitive prices, with the ultimate goal of satisfying consumers.

Incorporated in 2002, Coscharis Beverages is a world-class production organization with vested interest in water packaging and other non-alcoholic beverage production, complying with the highest health and safety standards. Our manufacturing outfits are located in Lagos and Anambra, from which our products are distributed nationwide.

As a distinctive hallmark of beverage production with more than a decade's track record, Coscharis Beverages has conceptualized customized beverages for companies, restaurants, ceremonies (parties, weddings & anniversaries), conventions and annual general meetings.

Why Our Yoghurt

Real milk, zero shortcuts.

Yoghurt is a dairy product made from fermented milk with various ingredients that provide health benefits, flavor and colour — among them stress relief, digestive aid, stronger bones and healthier skin.

Coscharis Yoghurt is made with real milk and Life Starter Culture under the right temperature for fermentation. All ingredients used are natural food grade, with zero chemical and synthetic additives — which is why we advise our Creamy Yoghurt be stored at room temperature or refrigerated. We ensure fresh batches are always produced and available for our customers.
