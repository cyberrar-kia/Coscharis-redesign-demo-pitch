Coscharis Technologies

Technologies

Distribution of reliable hardware and ICT service solutions for Nigeria's public and private sectors.

Through our distribution of reliable hardware and service solutions, Coscharis Technologies caters to niche information, communication and technology markets within Nigeria's public and private sectors.

The efficiency and integrity of our business practices over the years has facilitated CosTech in establishing enduring and robust global partnerships, thereby making us the customer's first choice to do business with.

With offices situated in Nigeria and Ghana, the journey towards Coscharis Technologies' vision — to become an everyday mention in the chatter of technology apologists within West Africa's market — picks up pace. As we proceed, our aim is to ensure we are constantly rewarding all stakeholders with value every step of the way.
