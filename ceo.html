Dr. Cosmas Maduka

The man who turned a one-man spare-parts business into Nigeria's most diversified indigenous conglomerate.

CON — COMMANDER OF THE ORDER OF THE NIGER
“

To build an institution that will be timeless in its relevance.

Dr. Cosmas Maduka is the founder, President and CEO of Coscharis Group. He started Coscharis Motors as a one-man business in 1977, importing and distributing genuine automotive spare parts, and over decades transformed it into an indigenous conglomerate with interests spanning manufacturing, ICT, petrochemicals, auto care and components, automobile sales and services, and agriculture.

Through his visionary leadership, Coscharis Motors secured the sole Nigerian franchise of BMW, MINI, Rolls-Royce and Jaguar Land Rover, and the Abro USA auto care and consumer products line, becoming a strong distribution partner for Ford in Nigeria along the way. He is an entrepreneur, motivational speaker and mentor to upcoming entrepreneurs across the country.

The Journey

From one man to a conglomerate.

Five decades of milestones, from a spare-parts stall to a Group with eight companies across two countries.

1977

One-man business

Begins importing and distributing genuine automotive spare parts — the seed of what would become Coscharis Group.

1993

Sole BMW franchise

Secures the sole Nigerian franchise for BMW, the deal that turns a spare-parts trader into an auto dealer.

2000–2012

Access Bank Plc

Serves 12 years as a Director of Access Bank Plc, one of Nigeria's leading banks.

2002

Coscharis Beverages

Incorporates Coscharis Beverages, moving the Group into water and dairy production.

2003

Honorary Doctorate

Awarded an Honorary Doctorate of Business Administration (PhD) by the University of Nigeria, Nsukka.

2012

Commander of the Order of the Niger

Conferred national honours (CON) by the President of the Federal Republic of Nigeria in September 2012.

2013

Rolls-Royce & MINI

Coscharis becomes only the second Rolls-Royce franchisee in Africa, adding MINI the same year.

2014

Coscharis Motors Assembly

Establishes CMAL to assemble the Ford Ranger in Ikeja — Nigeria's only Ford plant outside South Africa.

Nov 2015

Forbes Africa cover story

Featured on the cover of Forbes Africa, telling the story of building Coscharis from the ground up.

16 years

Nigerian Table Tennis Federation

Serves as President/Chairman, leading Nigeria's team to four Olympic Games — Atlanta '96, Athens 2000, Sydney 2004 and Beijing 2008.
