About Coscharis

Group Profile

A Nigerian conglomerate with businesses spanning automobile, medical, foods & beverages, information technology, engineering and agriculture.

Coscharis Group is a wholly owned Nigerian conglomerate with businesses and interests that span across various sectors of the Nigerian economy: Automobile, Medical, Foods & Beverages, Information Technology, Engineering and Agriculture. With globally respected partners and an iconic brand portfolio, Coscharis has continued to be a considerable contributor to Nigeria's economy.

Coscharis Group was birthed in 1977 with a modest beginning of importation and distribution of genuine automotive spare parts, accessories of Japanese vehicles and several kinds of automobile related products. This served as the foundation for which Coscharis would consolidate its automobile business, and also venture into various sectors of the economy — showcasing the leadership, business acumen and doggedness of the founder and President/CEO, Dr. Cosmas Maduka, CON.

With the visionary intent to "build an institution that will be timeless in its relevance," Coscharis Group proceeded into automobile sales as the sole representative of the BMW brand in Nigeria. Thereafter, the success of that partnership brought Jaguar Land Rover, Ford, MINI, Morris Garages and finally the luxurious Rolls-Royce brand into the Nigerian market — with Coscharis as only the second franchisee in the whole of Africa. Within the auto industry, Coscharis also holds the sole franchise of the Abro automotive care brands and the car rental franchise for Sixt Rent A Car through Coscharis Mobility.

The Group's interest spreads beyond automobiles through Coscharis Technology Ltd in the ICT sector, Coscharis Medical & Foods Ltd and CG Biostadt in healthcare, Coscharis Farms Ltd in agriculture — aiming to become one of the largest rice-producing farms in Nigeria — and the bottling and distribution of sachet and table water via Coscharis Beverages Ltd, established in 2002. In 2014, the Group entered vehicle manufacturing with the assembly of Ford Rangers through Coscharis Motors Assembly Ltd.

As an employer of labour, both local and international, it is Coscharis Group's duty to deliver world-class service and continually create value to its various stakeholders — regularly engaging in social responsibility investments such as the annual Week of Caring and Public Private Auto Training Programmes at its auto training centre.

Mission Statement
