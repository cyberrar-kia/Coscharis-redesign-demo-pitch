Coscharis Medical & Foods

Medical & Foods

Medical consultancy, diagnostic equipment and biomedical engineering support to hospitals since 1997.

Coscharis Medical & Foods Limited, established in 1997, operates as a professional service to healthcare. Since inception we have endeavored to be at the forefront in providing medical consultancy, pharmaceutical and diagnostic solutions to hospitals and homes.

Our business furnishes hospitals and other medical facilities with world-class modern equipment and consumables. With the support of our biomedical engineers, we provide excellent maintenance of medical and laboratory equipment to healthcare facilities, reinforcing our commitment to being with customers every step of the way.

Coscharis Medical provides ongoing training for medical personnel both locally and overseas, and has invested significantly in the training of medical personnel in minimally invasive surgeries and the use of modern technology. With offices in Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt and Enugu, Coscharis Medical & Foods leverages strong international relationships and vibrant local networks to deliver on our value promise.
