Dr. Cosmas Maduka

Founder, President & CEO, Coscharis Group

Dr. Cosmas Maduka is the founder, President/CEO of Coscharis Group. He started Coscharis Motors as a one-man business and, over the years, transformed the organization into an indigenous conglomerate with diverse interests in manufacturing, ICT, petrochemical, auto care and components, automobile sales and services, and agric and agro-allied sectors of the Nigerian economy.

Through his visionary leadership, Coscharis Motors secured sole franchise of BMW, MINI, Rolls-Royce and Jaguar Land Rover premium brands, and Abro USA auto care and consumer products, among others. He currently serves on the boards of CG-EKO LLP, First System Refinishes Ltd., CG Biostadt Limited and Sixt Rentals Nigeria, and served as a Director of Access Bank Plc for 12 years (2000–2012).

He served as President/Chairman of the Nigerian Table Tennis Federation for 16 years, leading the Nigerian team to the Olympic Games in Atlanta '96, Athens 2000, Sydney 2004 and Beijing 2008. Dr. Maduka is a motivational speaker and mentor to upcoming entrepreneurs, holds an Honorary Doctorate in Business Administration from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (2003), is an alumnus of Harvard Business School's Executive Education Program, and in September 2012 was conferred the National Honours of Commander of the Order of the Niger (CON).

Deputy Group Managing Director

Mr. Cosmas Jr. Maduka

Executive Director, Special Duties

Mr. Jonathan Maduka

Executive Director, Strategy

Appointed Executive Director in October 2014, charged with reform and restructuring across Coscharis Motors. Economics graduate of the University of Lagos and MBA holder from HULT International Business School, Boston.

Mr. Peter K. Maduka
