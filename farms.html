Coscharis Farms

Farms

2,500 hectares of rice cultivation in Anambra, working toward becoming one of Nigeria's largest rice producers.

Coscharis Farms, strategically located within Anambra — "light of our nation" — is home to hygienically fertilized and cultured rice grains, processed by in-house bio-experts into 100% Sortexed long-grain parboiled rice for mass market supply and consumption.

The farm currently spans 2,500 hectares of land, with an anticipated 5,000 hectares of out-growers scheme in the first phase of project implementation. With local consumption of rice in Anambra State estimated at 300,000 metric tons and present production at only 20,000 metric tons, Coscharis Farms aims to close that gap — producing about 20,000 metric tons of rice when the farm achieves full first-phase capacity, and expanding cultivation to 10,000 hectares with a target capacity of 70,000 metric tons in the second phase.

In conjunction with the Anambra State Government, Coscharis has developed a social impact model around the farms business — providing jobs and vocational training through the plantations while facilitating community development through construction of road networks linking the planting community and Anambra's central business district.
