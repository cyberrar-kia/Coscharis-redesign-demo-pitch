Coscharis Ghana

Ghana

Auto spare parts and the Abro auto care brand, distributed across four Ghanaian regions since 1992.

Coscharis Ghana Ltd was established in 1992, stemming from the strategic move of Coscharis Motors Ltd to build a strong distributive structure across the West African sub-region, for the importation and distribution of genuine automotive spare parts and accessories of Japanese vehicles alongside several kinds of automobile related products.

The company began operations in 1993 with distribution of auto spare parts, and it was the acquisition of the Abro franchise for Ghana, together with government approval to import lubricants, that launched Coscharis Ghana fully into the market. Coscharis Ghana has since made Abro the leading brand in the Auto Care industry in the country.

Today Coscharis Ghana is located on Spintex Road, Motorway Industrial Estate, Accra, and serves the Ghana market through a distributive structure of offices across four regions, with 9 depots and branches — aiming to boost accessibility of company products to customers and consumers in every part of the country at barely any cost to them.
